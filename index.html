How this works

What this app knows—

The simple version

This app uses Guna Milan (also called Ashtakoota), a well-known traditional Vedic astrology matching method. Your score out of 36 comes directly from a published South Indian marriage-points reference table, looked up by each partner's Moon Nakshatra and its pada (quarter). The 8-factor list below the score shows the traditional components of Guna Milan, computed separately using standard rules — regional reference tables can weight them slightly differently, so they may not always sum to exactly the same total.

Below that, the match also checks things astrologers traditionally look at alongside the 36-point score: Mangal Dosha (Manglik status, from the Moon chart), Nadi and Bhakoot Dosha with their commonly cited cancellation exceptions, the Rajju and Vedha dosha checks, and your Navamsha (D9) Moon sign with its Vargottama status. Different authors and regional traditions apply different or additional exceptions to these.

Adding a birthplace is optional but unlocks two more traditional reads: your Ascendant (Lagna) sign, and the fuller, Ascendant-based version of Mangal Dosha (generally considered more authoritative than the Moon-chart version alone). Birthplaces are matched against a city list bundled with the app itself — nothing you type is sent anywhere; if your city isn't listed, you can enter its latitude and longitude directly.

We estimate your Moon, Mars, and Ascendant positions from the date, time, timezone, and (optionally) birthplace you enter — you don't need your full birth chart. This is a simplified calculation, not professional astrology software, so if a placement falls right on the edge of a sign or Nakshatra, an astrologer using precise chart software could get a slightly different result. This app isn't a substitute for a full reading.
