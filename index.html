The simple version

This app uses Guna Milan (also called Ashtakoota), a well-known traditional Vedic astrology matching method. It checks 8 factors between two birth charts and adds them up to a score out of 36.

We estimate your Moon position from the date, time, and timezone you enter — you don't need your full birth chart. This is a simplified calculation, not professional astrology software, so if your Moon falls right on the edge of a sign or Nakshatra, an astrologer using precise chart software could get a slightly different result. This app doesn't apply extra exception rules some astrologers use, and it isn't a substitute for a full reading.
